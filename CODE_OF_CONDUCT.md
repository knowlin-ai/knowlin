# Code of Conduct

Knowlin / duckpilot follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**We pledge** to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone – regardless of age,
body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or
sexual identity and orientation.

**Please:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for
the community – and remember that many people here are hobbyists, students and kids with their first robot.

**Not okay:** harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks, publishing others'
private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a friendly workshop.

**Enforcement:** report problems **privately** to the maintainers: on GitHub via *Security → Report a vulnerability*
(start the title with “Conduct:”). Please don't report conduct problems in public issues. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, code or contributors that violate this code.
