Knowlin Link

One mind, many bodies. Connect your Reachy Mini to Knowlin – memory, character, conversation and spatial knowledge on your PC, shared with your other robots.

  1. Install this app from Reachy Mini Control → Applications → Discover Apps and start it.
  2. Install Knowlin on your PC: knowlin.de (Windows) or pipx install knowlin.
  3. In Knowlin choose Reachy Mini → Real robot and enter the address the app shows.

Knowlin also simulates Reachy Mini, so you can start before your robot arrives. The emergency stop switches the motors off immediately. Free, open source (Apache-2.0), not affiliated with Pollen Robotics or Hugging Face. Source: github.com/knowlin-ai/knowlin.
