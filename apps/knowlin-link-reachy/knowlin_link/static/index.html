Knowlin Link

Reachy Mini is ready for Knowlin. Enter this address in Knowlin under Reachy Mini → Real robot:

http://reachy-mini.local:8000
  1. Install Knowlin on your PC: knowlin.de or pipx install knowlin.
  2. Choose Reachy Mini, then Real robot, and paste the address.
  3. The emergency stop in Knowlin switches the motors off immediately.

This app does not move the robot. Knowlin is a free community project, not affiliated with Pollen Robotics or Hugging Face.
