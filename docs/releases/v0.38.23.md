# Knowlin 0.38.23 – Keynote-style training field, dancing by verse and chorus

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Training field like the keynote:** 144 ducks (40 on phones) on a wide field of practice tiles – flat, ramp, steps,
  waves, rough ground – with a glowing grid fading into the haze and a slowly gliding camera. When the original models
  are selected in the 3D view, the field uses them too (a light version with a few thousand triangles per duck, built
  once on your PC). On weak graphics it saves by itself.
- **Dancing by song structure:** the browser analysis now delivers a sound picture per bar (12 pitch classes, 6 bands).
  Boundaries, repeats and names (verse, chorus, bridge, breakdown, drop) come from it; the repeated loud part is the
  chorus even when it is quieter once. Tempo and downbeat are measured more precisely.
- **Moves that fit:** every chorus starts with its highlight, quiet phrases are danced smaller, slow songs are swayed.
- **Dance editor:** steps grouped by part, rename a part to re-plan it, “dance like the first one” copies a chorus.
- **Smaller network on a calm disc** on the start page, a flatter start camera, the Microduck colour also under
  Character → Personality, and a calmer, nicer hand display in Reachy's camera games (still never the camera image).
