# Knowlin 0.38.25 – Explored area and trail easy to see, training field in a grid, camera framed to the body

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Explored area easy to see:** the tiles stay, now light and cool on the wooden floor, obstacles in a stronger blue,
  no more flicker over the rug.
- **Walked trail fits in:** a soft light band like chalk on wood that fades out behind, instead of a thin line from dark
  grey to blue, slightly smoothed so each step's sway no longer shows.
- **Training field in a clear grid:** all practice fields parallel, row by row in opposite directions – like the keynote.
- **“Play” always findable:** the collision-mode button (place, push and throw things) now shows even without real
  physics; one click switches real physics on, a second click opens the mode.
- **Camera framed to the body:** start view, follow point and fly-in scale with the figure's height – Odi (Open Duck
  Mini) is fully in frame. Furniture right behind the camera turns see-through instead of making the camera jump close.
- **Odi avoids obstacles with its camera:** with a camera fitted, the Open Duck Mini estimates distances from the image
  (up to about 1.5 m) and walks around obstacles using the same avoidance layer as the Microduck.
- **Carried things look right:** the sock now looks like a sock (leg, heel, foot, stripes) and hangs from the beak by its cuff instead of sitting half inside the head, and the beak
  stays shut while talking when it holds something (it would drop it otherwise).
- **Honest actions:** the menu on a selected object no longer offers “pick up” or “bring it to me” for bodies without a
  beak (Open Duck Mini).
