# Knowlin 0.38.18 – Nicer in 3D, lighter to draw

*Deutsch unten.*

- **A cosier simulator room:** wooden floorboards with a faint 10 cm grid, dollhouse walls with skirting (the wall facing
  the camera steps back and lets clicks through), a rug under the coffee table, a calm gradient instead of black,
  furniture with softened edges and warmer light.
- **New simple embodiments** – our own designs, no CAD data – shown offline, before the first download and in this public
  version: the Microduck as a small robot with a visor, two camera eyes and an orange lower jaw; Open Duck Mini with its
  trapezoid head and antennas; Reachy Mini egg-shaped with glasses-like eyes.
- **Lighter original models:** when the models are built on your PC, CAD parts are simplified by surface area and merged
  per joint and material – a third fewer triangles for the Microduck, more than half fewer for Reachy Mini, with no
  visible difference up close. Reachy's body and head stay at full resolution: they feed the safety check that keeps the
  head out of the body.
- **Interface polish:** gzip for text, hover colours only with a mouse, buttons give slightly when pressed, and the
  emergency stop's accessible name now matches its visible label.

Not yet measured: frame rates on real GPUs and phones.
