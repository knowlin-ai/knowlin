# Knowlin 0.38.22 – Plexus and smooth surfaces

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Knowlin's network on the start page, rebuilt:** a spatial plexus like on the website – around three hundred neurons
  fill the whole brain shape, straight fine synapses, no outer shell. States, impulses and chain reactions stay.
- **Smooth surfaces on the original models:** normals are now averaged by corner angle instead of area, so the long thin
  triangles from the CAD export no longer streak across curved parts. Microduck, Reachy and Open Duck; existing models
  are re-baked once.

This version includes everything since 0.38.7 and is the new main version.

## Deutsch

Knowlins Netz als räumlicher Plexus (gefüllt, gerade Linien, keine Hülle) und glatte Flächen an den Originalmodellen
(Normalen nach Eckwinkel). Enthält alles seit 0.38.7 und ist der neue Hauptstand.
