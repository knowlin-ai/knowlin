# Knowlin 0.38.14 – A life of its own, hand-tracking games, content checked per body

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Keeps busy on its own:** without commands, the embodiment finds something to do after a while – matching its
  character and body. Calm characters wait longer and sometimes sit down; lively ones explore, dance or show tricks;
  Reachy looks around, plays with his antennas or turns towards things. On the real robot only gestures, never walking.
  Settings: off, now and then (default), often.
- **Camera games, extended:** hand detection (MediaPipe) loads only on click (≈ 14 MB, checksum-verified) and runs only
  in the browser. New games “Copy me” and “Rock, paper, scissors”; “Red light, green light” with levels, points and best
  time. Without a robot, Reachy in the simulator slides in next to the game card.
- **Voice design per body:** own presets and sample sentences for Reachy and the Open Duck.
- **Each embodiment's character is its own:** Knowlin's base character only affects Knowlin; the embodiments are points
  in Knowlin's field that you can drag individually (also with the arrow keys).
- **Settings show only what's needed** – e.g. fields for another language model appear when you choose one.
- **“Knowlin” instead of “duckpilot”** wherever you read it; texts per body (no duck words with Reachy); a permanent
  content check in the tests and the acceptance test.

## Deutsch

Ohne Befehle beschäftigt sich die Verkörperung jetzt von selbst, passend zum Charakter (am echten Roboter nur Gesten).
Reachys Kamera-Spiele haben Handerkennung (lädt erst auf Klick, rechnet nur im Browser), „Mach mir nach“ und „Schere,
Stein, Papier“ – ohne Roboter gleitet Reachy im Simulator neben die Spielkarte. Stimme entwerfen passt zu jedem Körper,
die Verkörperungen lassen sich im Knowlin-Feld einzeln verschieben, die Einstellungen zeigen nur, was gebraucht wird,
und überall steht Knowlin statt duckpilot.
