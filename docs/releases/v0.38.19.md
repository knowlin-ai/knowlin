# Knowlin 0.38.19 – Stage first

*Deutsch unten.*

- **A more refined interface:** in the cockpit the 3D stage fills the whole area and the conversation floats over it as a
  glass panel (from 1056 px; narrower screens stack as before). Warmer graphite, frosted glass for the header, rails and
  toasts, a display face (Bricolage Grotesque) for greetings, titles and posture, cards lit softly from above, pill tabs,
  a start page with a big greeting and a pill-shaped input.
- **Microduck in four colours, like Pollen's:** Cream, Graphite, Lavender, Sky – for the original model (only the light
  shells change, the orange stays) and the simple shape.
- **Pollen's apps for Reachy Mini from Knowlin:** start and stop the apps installed on your Reachy, install more from
  Pollen's catalogue on Hugging Face (two clicks, PC only). Knowlin uses Pollen's daemon; the app runs on the Reachy.
  While an app runs Knowlin sends no targets of its own, and the emergency stop stops the app too. In the simulator the
  page says honestly that apps need the real robot.
- **Training field:** 50 small ducks practise at once on ramps, steps and waves; whoever falls starts again. Without a
  run it shows a time-lapse learning curve; with a run, as many ducks stay standing as the run's measured share of
  episodes without a fall. Four draw calls however many ducks, and it only renders while visible.

Not yet tested: Pollen's apps on a real Reachy Mini (tested against a replica of the reachy-mini 1.13 daemon); frame
rates of the training field and glass on real GPUs and phones.
