# Knowlin 0.38.7 – one mind, many bodies

*Deutsch unten.*

**Knowlin** is a shared brain for small robots: one body of knowledge about your surroundings, your everyday life and
shared experiences – and for each robot its own character with a name, voice and personality. This release brings the
Microduck module **duckpilot** together with **Reachy Mini** and the **Open Duck Mini v2** as further bodies.

> Free, non-commercial hobby project – not affiliated with Pollen Robotics, Hugging Face or the Open Duck project.
> Pollen's robot models are not included; Knowlin downloads them from the original source and prepares them on your PC.

## Highlights

- **Starts in the head.** The start page shows what all characters share and the characters themselves. Wake one and
  you enter its body – with a short “mind to body” transition.
- **Three bodies:** Microduck (simulator, real physics, real robot via `robotd`), Reachy Mini (original model,
  Pollen's emotions and dances; real robot via Pollen's daemon, untested), Open Duck Mini v2 (open model, simulator).
- **Every body shows only what it can do** – interface, brain and skills follow one rule.
- **Characters:** own name, voice, way of speaking and tricks per body; shared knowledge, surroundings, everyday life.
- **Knowlets:** share and load a character's personality, tricks and dances – data only, never knowledge about people.
- **Safety:** emergency stop always wins, 2-minute lock afterwards, secrets only in the system vault, people only on
  the PC with consent, paid training only with two clicks.

## Install

Windows: double-click `start.bat` (Python 3.11+). macOS/Linux: `./start.sh`. Details: [README](../../README.en.md).

## Known limits

- Real robots are untested with Knowlin so far (Microduck deliveries start in December); reports are very welcome –
  use the issue template “Report from a real robot”.
- The interface is German and English; server-side status messages are partly German only.
- The Open Duck Mini v2 runs only in the simple simulator.
