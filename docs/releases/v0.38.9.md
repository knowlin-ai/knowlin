# Knowlin 0.38.9 – the mind itself

*Deutsch unten.*

Knowlin on the start page now speaks as itself: calm, serious and a little mysterious – “an intelligent, learning life
form” – with its own, deeper voice. Only inside a body does it become a specific character.

## Highlights

- **Ask first, then go into a body.** For physical requests (“dance”, “walk forward”, “show me a roll”) Knowlin names
  the body it needs and asks whether to use it. Say “yes” (or press “Use Quacki”) and it goes into the character and
  passes the request on; say “no” and it stays without a body. It only proposes bodies that can really do it.
- **Its own deep voice** – calm, no duck filter, never silly (Piper “Thorsten” if installed, otherwise the installed
  Piper voice or the browser voice, both lowered).
- **Names:** give Knowlin a name and tell it yours in the conversation (“I'll call you Nova”, “My name is …”) or set
  both under Settings → Knowlin. Until then: “Hello human”. Your name stays on your PC.

## Known limits

- The deep Piper voice is covered by tests (length, pitch) but has not been listened to on a PC yet.
