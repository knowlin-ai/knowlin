# Knowlin 0.38.26 – Appearance with previews, Hub tricks with stills, spacing checked

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Appearance with previews:** each of the four colours shows your own Microduck in that colour, rendered in the browser
  from the model on stage.
- **Hub tricks with stills:** every tile shows a still from its preview video as soon as it scrolls into view (only the
  start is loaded, at most three at a time); hovering plays it muted, a click loops it. Without a video it says so.
- **Spacing:** the body-trait switches now have the same breathing room and separators as every other switch list, and the
  acceptance test checks the spacing of switches, buttons and fields on desktop and phone.

## Deutsch

Aussehen mit Vorschaubildern je Farbe, Hub-Tricks mit Standbild (lädt beim Hinsehen, höchstens drei gleichzeitig),
Abstand der Gestalt-Schalter behoben, und die Abnahme prüft Abstände am PC und am Handy.
