# Knowlin 0.38.21 – To it, around it, and nicer

*Deutsch unten.*

- **“Walk around the table”:** once all the way round with clearance, head turned towards the target, left or right.
  If something stands against a wall, she turns back and walks the free sides – and says so honestly.
- **Odi kicks** in the simulator – re-enacted; the real Open Duck has no learned kick yet.
- **Drop next to things:** “bring them to the ball” puts them next to the ball.
- **Searching like a living thing:** look all the way round, go to the last sighting or a little way in a new direction,
  look again – up to three places, then an honest “not found”.
- **Original models at full resolution again:** the simplification from 0.38.18 made printed brackets look crumpled.
  Visible parts stay whole; only hidden parts are simplified. Merging per joint keeps draw calls low.
- **Reachy's games refined:** calmer picker; while playing, the stage comes first with the game as a glass panel.
- **Fixed:** “walk to the ball” without Claude only walked 40 cm ahead; the duck view was blocked for Odi and the simple
  shapes.
