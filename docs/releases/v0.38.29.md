# Knowlin 0.38.29 – Try Odi in your browser

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Try Odi:** a new page, `knowlin.de/ausprobieren.html`. Odi (Open Duck Mini v2, Apache-2.0) runs entirely in the
  browser – no install, no account, no AI key, no requests to other servers. Walk with the arrows or WASD, sit, nod,
  shake the head, look around, dance, go to the ball, or type a few words in German or English.
- Odi only loads on click (about 4 MB). Same 3D view and same gait formula as Knowlin's simple simulator. Pollen's
  models never go on the website.
