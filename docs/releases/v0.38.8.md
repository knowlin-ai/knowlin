# Knowlin 0.38.8 – Hello human

*Deutsch unten.*

The start page becomes a quick start. At the top, Knowlin itself greets you – **“Hello human”** – with a text field
right below: type or hold the microphone button and speak. The answer comes from the shared mind behind all
characters: calm, no duck sounds, with its own conversation and the same AI model as the characters. Next to the
dialog the mind is drawn as a soft, shimmering shape with synapses that shows whether it is resting, listening,
thinking or speaking.

## Highlights

- **Talk to the mind directly** – no character has to be awake. Without an AI model it still understands simple
  sentences (greeting, “what's your name?”, “what can you do?”, timers, time of day).
- **Wake a character from the conversation:** “wake Quacki”, “Reachy wecken” or one click on the suggestion. Ask for
  something physical (“dance”) and the mind offers the character whose body can do it.
- **Safety stays the same:** “stop” halts every body immediately, also from the start page. The mind has no body
  tools; waking a character only works on the PC.
- The shape only animates while visible and stands still with “reduce motion”.

## Known limits

- The mind speaks with the voice of the last awake character for now (without the duck filter).
- Mind with Ollama or other providers is covered by tests only.

## Deutsch

Die Startseite wird zum Schnelleinstieg: Oben begrüßt dich Knowlin selbst – **„Hallo Mensch“** –, darunter das
Dialogfeld zum Schreiben oder Sprechen. Es antwortet der gemeinsame Kopf aller Figuren, ruhig und ohne Entenlaute.
Links daneben wabert der Kopf als weiche Form mit Synapsen und zeigt, ob er ruht, zuhört, nachdenkt oder spricht. Für
alles Körperliche weckt er auf Wunsch eine Figur – mit demselben Übergang wie auf den Kacheln. „Stopp“ wirkt weiter
sofort auf jeden Körper. Alle Änderungen: [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).
