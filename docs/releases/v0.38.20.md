# Knowlin 0.38.20 – A clear emergency stop

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Emergency stop redesigned:** a clear red button with a white stop sign instead of the industrial mushroom on a
  yellow dashed plate. While something is running, a solid red ring breathes softly to show that Esc will stop it now
  (calm with reduced motion). Same on the PC, in the phone's bottom bar and in the phone controller; its accessible
  name is unchanged.
- **Fixed:** on phones, content showed through the fixed bottom bar (since 0.38.19) – it is dense glass now.

roter Ring. Am Handy deckt die Leiste unten den Inhalt wieder ab.
