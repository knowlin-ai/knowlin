# Knowlin 0.38.12 – spatial, calm synapses

*Deutsch unten.*

- **The synapses at Knowlin look spatial:** nodes as lit spheres, distant ones fade into haze, and two faint orbits turn
  with the network.
- **Activity glows calmly instead of flashing;** impulses are rarer and slower.
- **Plainer wording:** Knowlin says it uses one of its robots when something needs movement.
