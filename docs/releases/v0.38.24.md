# Knowlin 0.38.24 – Practice fields, restore points, Knowlin wakes up, emergency stop cuts power

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Restore points – like life, but with a way back:** Settings → Restore points. Knowlin makes one every day and
  another before every reset, restore or “Forget everything”. Create your own, reset single areas (memory, experience
  network, character, exercises, dances, room), restore an earlier state completely or area by area, export it as a ZIP
  and import it again. Reset, restore and delete need a second click. PC only; people, keys and settings are never
  included.
- **Training field with full models and a real gait:** the simplified meshes had holes and the original models walked
  sideways. Now the figure is drawn once in eight gait phases from 16 directions at full resolution, and every duck on
  the field is an aligned picture – as light as 144 rectangles for the graphics card. Joint angles come from the
  simulator, step rate and speed match, ducks face where they walk. Falls work like in real training: stumble, tip
  over, lie briefly, then the world resets and the duck starts again.
- **Every duck on its own practice field** (like NVIDIA's Microduck picture): a bright hall with a grid floor and 144
  separate fields – wooden ramp, steps, platform, waves or a flat mat, each turned its own way. Every duck walks its
  field from start to end and never wanders onto its neighbours'; harder ground means more falls.
- **Knowlin wakes up:** instead of a start image, sparks flow into the Knowlin sign until the connection is ready,
  then it lights up – “Knowlin is awake”, just like moving into a body. The public app icons now show the Knowlin sign.
- **Emergency stop cuts power:** button, Esc, controller or “stop” cancel everything and switch the motors off at once –
  `robot.relax` on the Microduck, motors disabled on the Reachy Mini, a slump in the simulator, extra ducks included.
- **Team with up to eight extra ducks** chosen with a number row instead of a drop-down, with a live preview next to it.
- **Cleaner cockpit:** no small duck-view picture hidden behind the conversation, no empty “turn” gauge on Reachy, nothing
  overlaps at 1440, 1100 or 390 px – now part of the automatic acceptance test.
- **The field says what it is:** “Preview – nothing learns here” or “Measured rate, motion re-enacted”, plus one
  sentence on where learning actually happens (a started run, on the PC or on Kaggle).
- **Every start begins with one duck;** extra ducks stay until you quit and wear random Pollen colours – yours keeps
  the colour you chose, and the training field shows only your duck. **Brain activity without circles.**
