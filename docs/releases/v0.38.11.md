# Knowlin 0.38.11 – quiet Reachy, character field, living synapses

*Deutsch unten.*

- **Knowlin's base character as a field with quadrants** – calm ↔ lively, serious ↔ playful, with the embodiments shown
  as small points around it. Works with mouse, touch and arrow keys.
- **Living synapses at Knowlin:** under Knowledge → Memory the experience network slowly turns by itself, nodes float,
  impulses travel along the connections and a soft membrane wobbles behind it. In a body it stays calm; “reduce motion”
  stops it.
- **Fixed:** Reachy Mini made duck sounds while idle; duck chatter could be heard while you were with Knowlin.

## Deutsch

Knowlins Grundcharakter jetzt als Feld mit Quadranten, die Verkörperungen als Punkte darum herum. Die Synapsen leben bei
Knowlin (drehen, schweben, Impulse, wabernde Hülle). Behoben: Reachy quakte im Leerlauf; bei Knowlin schnatterte die
Ente im Hintergrund.
